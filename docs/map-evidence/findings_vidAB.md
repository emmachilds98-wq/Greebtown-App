# Boomtown Fair 2026 — Map Evidence Findings (vidA_0802_1516 + vidB_0802_1552)

Source note: vidA and vidB both pan/zoom around the same central cluster of the
official Boomtown app map (Mapbox-based). Coverage in both videos is heavily
weighted to the Botanica / Metropolis / Area 404 / Oldtown / Grand Central /
Quantum / Hilltop / The Lion's Den core, with a couple of excursions further
out (Full Moon Ballroom area, an "Observatory/Retreat/Elemental/Circus"
cluster, Hidden Woods, and a wide zoomed-out shot showing surrounding roads).

**Important caveat**: `vidB` frames 36-48 are NOT the official Boomtown app.
They are a brief app-switcher / preview capture of this project's own
in-progress app (visibly labelled "Greebtown!" in the iOS app switcher, frame
`f_0037.jpg`), showing a schematic legend-based map (District area / Camping
field / Parking / Woods / Stage / Other stage / Named hidden venue / Gate /
Landmark). It already has pins and drawn paths for Nexus, Helix, Hidden Woods,
Grand Central, The Lion's Den, Anara Forest, Oldtown, Thrutopia, Copperwood,
Area 404, Metropolis, Hydro XL, Botanica/Letsbe Avenue. **This is the current
app's own (possibly already-wrong/scraped-GPS-derived) data being reflected
back, not new confirmation from official footage.** It is reported below in
its own section, clearly separated, for reference only - do not use it to
overwrite hand-placed coordinates without independent official-app
confirmation.

## Districts / places - facts from OFFICIAL APP footage

### Botanica (district, top-left/west area of map)
- "BOTANICA" stylised green-text district label sits at the top of a cluster
  of orange/brown venue-chip labels that loop in a rough backwards-C / horseshoe
  shape. (vidA f_0060, f_0070, f_0091; vidB f_0022, f_0035)
- **Letsbe Avenue** sits immediately north/above Botanica, connected as if the
  entry path into Botanica (dark stylised label "LETSBE AVENUE"). (vidA f_0006,f_0060,f_0088; vidB f_0035,f_0039)
- **NEXUS**: yellow glowing label sitting on/at the base of a dark triangular
  mound/tent shape, roughly in the middle-west of Botanica, south-west of
  "Botanica Zoo" and "Karma Ceuticals". (vidA f_0060 - cropped crops/f0060_botanica.png)
- **Botanica Zoo**: venue-chip label north-east of Nexus, inside Botanica's
  eastern lobe. (vidA f_0060)
- **Karma Ceuticals**: chip label directly below/south of Botanica Zoo, same
  eastern lobe. (vidA f_0060)
- **Sub Lab**: chip label south of Nexus/the toilet block, toward the middle
  of the Botanica loop. (vidA f_0060)
- Botanica's loop path continues south into **Metropolis** and **Area 404**
  (all three sit in the same wooded bowl/enclosure - see path section).

### Hidden Woods (camping-style area)
- Pale/tan rectangular clearing with small square "pod"/tent icons, sitting
  immediately **north-west of Botanica**, above/left of Letsbe Avenue.
  Labelled "HIDDEN WOODS" in a dark rounded label. (vidA f_0088; vidB f_0030,f_0039)
- Sits inside/adjacent to a field separately signed **"DOWNTOWN CAMPING"**
  (orange campsite-style label) just south-west of it, and **"WEST CAMPING"**
  further north-west near the Alresford Rd boundary. (vidB f_0052)

### Metropolis (district, south of Botanica)
- "METROPOLIS" large stylised multicolour label sits south of Botanica,
  west side of the map, directly north-west of **Area 404**. (vidA f_0065,f_0070,f_0091,f_0093)
- **Hydro XL**: purple-glow stylised label south-west of Metropolis, at the
  far west edge of the whole site, connected by a path running down/left out
  of the main cluster. (vidA f_0065,f_0091,f_0093; also present unlabelled purple glow bottom-left in many frames)
- **The Hide Out (Downtown)**: pale label ("THE HIDE OUT DOWNTOWN") sitting in
  the gap between Metropolis and Area 404, south of both. Distinguishes it
  from the separate "The Hide Out Hilltop" (see below). (vidA f_0065,f_0070)

### Area 404 (district/stage, red/green glitch-style label)
- Bold "AREA 404" glitch-styled label, east of Metropolis, south-east of
  Botanica's eastern lobe. (vidA f_0001,f_0060,f_0070, many others)
- **Spectrum 360**: a distinctive robot/mascot-shaped stage structure with a
  yellow "SPECTRUM 360" chip label directly **north of Area 404**, surrounded
  by magenta/pink glowing decorative shapes. (vidA f_0001 - cropped crops/f0001_spectrum.png)
- Area 404's purple/magenta stage-glow icon (same Spectrum 360 structure)
  recurs consistently just north of the "AREA 404" text in nearly every wide
  shot, confirming the relative position.

### Grand Central (district)
- Red-background stylised "GRAND CENTRAL" label, north-east of Oldtown, at
  the top of the Oldtown "high street" run of venue chips. Consistently the
  first landmark encountered heading north from Oldtown. (many frames, e.g. vidA f_0005,f_0035,f_0088)
- An orange/red glowing rectangular stage sits immediately south-west of the
  Grand Central text (its actual stage structure). (vidA f_0035,f_0055)

### Oldtown (district, dark red gothic-styled label)
- "OLDTOWN" gothic-script label sits roughly mid-way down a long north-south
  strip of venue chips that starts at Grand Central (north) and runs down to
  Tribe of Frog / Quantum (south). (vidA f_0009,f_0030,f_0055, etc.)
- Confirmed sub-venue chip labels inside Oldtown's strip (legible after
  zoom, vidA f_0030/crops/f0030_oldtown.png):
  - **La Luna Coven** - west column, mid-height
  - **The Common Ground** - centre column, roughly level with La Luna Coven
  - **The Feckless Wrecked** - east column, level with/just below "the common ground"
  - A red-bordered label in the top-right of the Oldtown strip reading
    something like "...FOR (G)OLD TOWN" (consistent with the known name
    "Mining for (g)Old Town") - partially cropped off-frame, not fully legible.
  - Several other chip labels present in the same strip are present but too
    low-resolution to read confidently even after 3x crop (source video
    resolution is the limiting factor, not crop technique).
- **Tribe of Frog**: orange chip label at the south end of the Oldtown strip,
  just before Quantum, with its own small clearing/path loop. (vidA f_0002,f_0030,f_0055)
- **Quantum**: purple stylised label at the very bottom of the Oldtown strip,
  where it meets the path fork toward The Lion's Den / Hilltop / Sunset Hill.
  (vidA f_0005 and almost every subsequent Oldtown-area frame)

### Hilltop (camping field, yellow fill)
- Large yellow diagonal ground-fill polygon immediately **east of
  Oldtown/Quantum**, with a yellow warning-triangle "HILLTOP" campsite marker
  inside it. (vidA f_0005,f_0010,f_0016; vidB f_0010,f_0021)
- A second, smaller yellow fill area labelled **"Sunset"** (Sunset campsite)
  sits further south, near the bottom edge of the map close to Petersfield Rd.
  (vidA f_0005,f_0011; separate from the "Sunset Hill" venue chip, which
  is a plain orange label west of Quantum, not inside the yellow zone.)

### The Lion's Den (stage)
- Warm orange-glow stage structure (long low building) sitting **south-east
  of the Hilltop yellow zone**, east of Quantum, with dense tree cover to its
  south. Consistently the easternmost named stage in the whole cluster.
  (vidA f_0005,f_0010,f_0012,f_0016; vidB f_0010,f_0016,f_0021)
- Immediately east of The Lion's Den (further east/south-east) is a large
  red/pink camping field with confetti-style ground texture (a
  "residential"/campervan-style field, unlabelled in these frames). (vidA f_0009,f_0010)

### The Hide Out - Hilltop / Full Moon Ballroom cluster
- A separate "THE HIDE OUT HILLTOP" white pale label sits on a tan/brown
  hill-shaped mound, in open grassland **north-west of a white oval
  dome/tent structure** labelled **"FULL MOON BALLROOM"**. (vidA f_0045 -
  cropped crops/f0045_hideout.png) - first confirmed sighting of Full Moon
  Ballroom in this evidence set.
  - South of Full Moon Ballroom (i.e. further into the clearing) is a chip
    labelled **"VELVET ROPE"**.
  - East of The Hide Out Hilltop mound is **"Foggers Mill"**, marked with a
    white arrow-shaped ground marker beside it.
  - Two smaller unclear chip labels sit between The Hide Out Hilltop and Full
    Moon Ballroom (reads roughly "WET FACTORY" and one partially obscured,
    possibly a talent-agency-style joke name) - too pixelated to confirm
    precisely.
  - This whole cluster sits in an isolated grassy clearing, reached only via
    the same "Temple Valley Camping" side of the map (top-right in the wide
    shots), i.e. north-east of Grand Central / Oldtown, not directly
    connected to the Botanica/Metropolis loop in these frames.

### "Observatory / Retreat / Elemental / Circus" cluster (unlabelled district)
- A cluster of pale-labelled venues in sparse woodland, appears to sit
  **north of Grand Central / Oldtown**, in the same general direction as
  Temple Valley Camping. (vidA f_0083-f_0086)
  - **The Observatory**: white pale label on a tan/brown plot, west side of
    this cluster. (f_0084,f_0085 - cropped crops/f0085_a.png)
  - **The Retreat**: white pale label on a matching tan plot, east side of
    the same clearing. (f_0085 - cropped crops/f0085_b.png)
  - **Elemental**: orange/red plot with white pale label, south of
    Observatory/Retreat. (f_0085 - cropped crops/f0085_c.png)
  - **Circus**: white pale label just south of Elemental. (f_0085 -
    cropped crops/f0085_d.png)
  - The Observatory and The Retreat sit on opposite sides of a strip of open
    grass with scattered trees; Elemental and Circus sit further south/below
    that strip. Exact adjacency to named districts (Botanica/Grand Central)
    could not be pinned down more precisely than "north of Grand Central,
    same broad area as Temple Valley Camping" from these frames.

### Camping fields / ground-fill zones (confirmed labels)
- **Tangerine Fields** - orange label, north of the main cluster, near
  Valley Camping. (vidA f_0005,f_0006)
- **Valley Camping** - orange label, adjacent to Tangerine Fields, north edge
  of map. (vidA f_0005,f_0006; vidB f_0015 "VALLEY CAMPING")
- **Temple Valley Camping** - top-right corner label, north-east of Grand
  Central. (vidA f_0006,f_0009,f_0011)
- **West Camping** - far north-west corner label. (vidB f_0052)
- **Downtown Camping** - orange label just west of Hidden Woods/Botanica.
  (vidB f_0052)
- **Site of Special Scientific Interest** - plain text label (not a venue),
  appears twice, in open green fields north-west and south of the main
  cluster - a real-world SSSI designation shown on the base map, not a
  festival venue. (vidA f_0006,f_0035,f_0091; vidB f_0030)
- Two large pink/red confetti-textured fields (likely residential/campervan
  camping) sit: (1) directly east of The Lion's Den, and (2) at the far
  west edge of the site near a small dark triangular icon. Both visible in
  the full-site zoom-out (vidA f_0095,f_0096,f_0097).

### Roads / real-world orientation (from full zoom-out, vidA f_0095-f_0097)
- **Alresford Rd (A31)** runs along the **north** edge of the site,
  east-west, with **Avington Rd** branching north from a junction roughly
  above the Botanica/Grand Central boundary.
- **Petersfield Rd (A272)** runs along the **south** edge of the site,
  curving from south-west to south-east; **B3404** joins it at a roundabout
  at the site's south-west corner.
- The whole festival footprint is a rounded, roughly pentagon/kite shape
  sitting in the wedge between these two roads, with Botanica/Metropolis/
  Area 404 in the west lobe, Grand Central/Oldtown as the north-south spine,
  and Hilltop/Lion's Den/Sunset as the east lobe.

## Paths observed (official-app footage only)

- **Oldtown strip** runs roughly north-south: Grand Central (north end) ->
  Oldtown (middle) -> Tribe of Frog (near south end) -> forks at Quantum
  (south end) into two branches: one heading south-west to Sunset Hill, one
  heading south-east skirting the Hilltop yellow zone down to The Lion's Den.
- A dark thin diagonal line (looks like a fence/boundary rather than a
  footpath) runs along the west edge of the Hilltop yellow zone, parallel to
  the Oldtown strip, visible in nearly every mid-zoom frame (e.g. vidA
  f_0009,f_0016,f_0055).
- **Botanica <-> Metropolis <-> Area 404**: all three sit inside one continuous
  wooded enclosure with a horseshoe/loop-shaped internal path visible as the
  string of venue chips curling from Letsbe Avenue down through Botanica,
  looping south through Metropolis, and back east through Area 404 (visible
  as the continuous chip-lined "street" in vidA f_0070,f_0091,f_0093).
- **Hidden Woods** connects south-east into this same enclosure via a single
  path/track past the "Downtown Camping" field (vidB f_0030,f_0039).
- A pale/white unpaved track (light tan, distinct from the darker green
  woodland) runs along the west boundary of the whole site, visible curving
  from north (near Botanica) down to the south-west corner (vidA f_0035,
  f_0093), likely a perimeter/access road rather than a public path.
- The Hide Out (Hilltop)/Full Moon Ballroom clearing (vidA f_0045) is not
  shown connected by any visible path within the captured frames - it
  appears reached from off the top/right edge of that frame, consistent
  with lying toward Temple Valley Camping.
