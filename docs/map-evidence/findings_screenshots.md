# Boomtown Fair 2026 — Official App Map Evidence Findings (screenshots batch)

Several files (001, 008, 009, 013, 023, 024, 043) are low-res contact sheets containing many small thumbnails rather than one screenshot — used only for corroboration, not fine positional detail. Two images (`shot_040`-`shot_042`, `shot_049`) are the app's own **schematic/legend overview map** (District area / Camping field / Stage / Other stage / Named hidden venue / Gate / Landmark legend) — these are the highest-value evidence because they show the path network and stage markers explicitly, not just ground-fill shapes.

## District-level layout (from schematic overview, shot_040/041/042/049 — most authoritative)

- Core path network hub is **OLDTOWN** (a junction, not a stage itself): paths radiate from it to Grand Central (west/northwest), Copperwood/Area 404 (north), Anara Forest (north-east, via the Copperwood corridor), and The Lion's Den (south).
- **GRAND CENTRAL** (stage) sits inside its own district polygon west of Oldtown, connected to the Copperwood/Area 404 junction node by a single path.
- **AREA 404** and **COPPERWOOD** district labels sit side-by-side in the same row on the schematic (Area 404 west/left, Copperwood east/right), both south of **THRUTOPIA** and both feeding the same path junction that also connects to Grand Central, Oldtown, and Anara Forest.
- **THRUTOPIA** district label sits directly north of **COPPERWOOD**, and its district outline (teal dashed boundary) overlaps/abuts a camping-field polygon that also touches **ANARA FOREST**'s western edge. Thrutopia is east of the Nexus/Botanica/Letsbe Avenue cluster and north-west of Anara Forest. — **previously unconfirmed name, now located.**
- **ANARA FOREST** has its own stage (green triangle) at the top/north end of a wooded polygon; the polygon's southern point connects via path down to the Oldtown junction. Anara Forest sits east of Copperwood.
- **HIDDEN WOODS** stage sits inside its own district polygon (yellow/olive, star-shaped orange outline) directly north of the Nexus/Botanica/Letsbe Avenue cluster.
- **NEXUS** (stage) sits on the path between **METROPOLIS** (west of it) and the Botanica/Letsbe Avenue cluster (east/north-east of it), directly south of Hidden Woods.
- **HELIX** (stage) sits just south/south-west of Nexus, between Nexus and **HYDRO XL** (labelled "XL" at the edge of frame, further west/south-west), on a path branch that also leads toward Grand Central. **Previously-unconfirmed stage — now located**: south-west quadrant of the map, between Metropolis/Hydro XL and Grand Central.
- **THE LION'S DEN** (stage, orange glow marker) sits south of Oldtown, with a camping field (dotted/hex pattern polygon, distinct from Hilltop) immediately to its east. A gate/parking icon sits further south along the same path.

## AREA 404

- Neighbours METROPOLIS directly; Area 404 is east/south-east of Metropolis's arch, with **THE HIDE OUT (DOWNTOWN)** sitting in the gap between them, slightly south.
- Contains/adjacent to (all inside the Area 404 orange glow cluster): **SPECTRUM 360** (orange glow venue, purple/teal flags, top-right of the sub-cluster), **HANGER 161**, **DEVIANT LOUNGE** (directly south of Hanger 161), **BBXL** (south-east of Deviant Lounge), **ACID LEAK** (diamond plot, south-east corner).
- **HYDRO (XL)** sits south-west of Area 404/Metropolis, at the edge of the developed area, with a distinct purple/magenta ground-fill halo and what looks like a small pond immediately south of it.

## METROPOLIS

- Sits between Botanica (north) and Area 404 (east); Hydro XL is south-west of it.
- **NEXUS** sits at Metropolis's north-west corner/edge, a dark triangular "night market"-style shape with an orange glow marker.

## BOTANICA

- Central-north district; **LETSBE AVENUE** sits directly north of Botanica, connected by a path curving down into the Botanica loop.
- Contains, clustered along its internal path loop: **BOTANICA ZOO**, **KARMA CEUTICALS**, **MANGO** (faint, west side), **THE NETWORK**, **SUB LAB**, **NACHTLICKER**, a laundrette label consistent with **SOAPRANOS LAUNDRETTE**, and **ROSE AND CLOWN** (east side, near a Toilet icon).
- **HIDDEN WOODS** sits north-west of Botanica/Letsbe Avenue, across an open green field, its own small polygon with light dot-pattern ground fill and a stage marker.

## COPPERWOOD (HEIGHTS)

- North-east of Grand Central/Oldtown; on-map label reads "COPPERWOOD HEIGHTS" in most shots.
- A red-bordered label sits directly north of Copperwood Heights, at the edge of a dense-tree/forest polygon. Text reads "TANGLED WOODS" in one close crop (shot_023) but reads more like "TANGLED ROOTS" in adjacent wider shots — inconsistent/blurry at this zoom level; treat the exact spelling as unconfirmed, but the location is firm: immediately north of Copperwood Heights, at the forest boundary.
- **THE HIDE OUT HILLTOP** venue label sits at Copperwood Heights' east edge.

## GRAND CENTRAL

- Directly north of Oldtown; distinct orange/red glow marker and district fill separate from surrounding green. A path curves from Grand Central down into Oldtown.
- Contains, in the immediate vicinity: **ANCIENT FUTURES** (east side, orange label near a food/drink cluster), a "Downtown Hall"-style label, a "Daily Rag"-style label at its south exit path.
- **REBEL GIRLS CLUB** and **CIRCUS** (large circular red/orange plot) sit together in an otherwise empty green field — most likely within/adjacent to the Grand Central or Copperwood district (captured in the same session as Grand Central close-ups).

## OLDTOWN

- Major path junction directly south of Grand Central, north of The Lion's Den/Quantum, west of the Hilltop camping zone.
- Venues clustered around the "OLDTOWN" text label: **THE FOOLS LEAP** (west side), **DEN OF DIS ORDER** (north-east of the text, toward Grand Central), **POSTAL POSSE** and **TROUGH LOVE** (west/south-west side), **DAILY RAG** and a wifi "TOP UP" point (north edge, toward Grand Central), **DA GRAAFF'S REFORMATORY** (south-west corner, furthest from Grand Central).
- **THE FECKLESS WRECKED** and **SÍBÍN BEAG** sit together on the east/right side of the Oldtown cluster: in the same small-panel crop, "SÍBÍN BEAG" is the upper label and "THE FECKLESS WRECKED" sits immediately below/south of it, with "DEN OF DIS ORDER" further north in the same column. This places **Síbín Beag on the east side of Old Town, between Den of Dis Order (north of it) and The Feckless Wrecked (south of it)** — the opposite side of the Oldtown label from The Fools Leap/Postal Posse/Trough Love/Da Graaff's Reformatory, which are all on the west side. This is the most precise fix available for a **previously-unconfirmed venue**.
- **TRIBE OF FROG** sits south-west of Oldtown/south of Old Town's venue cluster, its own fenced/pathed compound with an orange/purple glow marker, west of **QUANTUM**.

## QUANTUM

- Sits directly south of Old Town/Tribe of Frog, at the western edge of the **HILLTOP** yellow camping zone; a purple-outlined stylised "QUANTUM" label marks a path junction here.

## THE LION'S DEN

- South-east of Quantum, east of Hilltop's southern tip; marked by a distinct orange glow over a stage-shaped icon. A small blue landmark/water icon sits just north-east of it.
- A red/pink camping-field polygon sits directly east/south-east of The Lion's Den, sharing a boundary.

## HILLTOP (yellow camping zone)

- Large yellow polygon running roughly north-south, west edge borders Old Town/Grand Central's district, north tip near Grand Central, south end tapers toward Quantum/The Lion's Den. Marked with a "camp fire" diamond warning icon and "HILLTOP" text partway down.
- Distinct from a second yellow zone labelled **SUNSET** (own diamond icon) further south near the A272/Petersfield Rd, separated from Hilltop by a strip of green district (Sunset Hill/Tribe of Frog area).

- **ANARA** (pink stylised text) forest/meadow area, with **TEMPLE VALLEY CAMPING** at its eastern edge; scattered red/blue/orange diamond pitch markers fill the field.
- **EAST CAMPING**, **EAST GATE**, **CAMPERVAN FIELD** labels appear together moving further east/south-east from Anara, along the perimeter road.
- **VALLEY CAMPING** label appears north of Old Town/Grand Central, along Alresford Rd, in a services area distinct from Temple Valley Camping.
- West/entrance side: **WEST** (orange banner), **DOWNTOWN** (camping) and **MEADOW** (camping) labels appear together near Petersfield Rd, with a pink/red camping-field triangle and a "CAMPLIGHT" point-of-interest.
- A second, separate red/pink camping polygon (distinct shape from the Anara-area one) appears at the extreme west edge in several wide shots bordering a road, likely a different camping field from the Hilltop-area pink zone.

## Roads / orientation cues

- **Alresford Rd** runs along the north edge of the site.
- **Petersfield Rd / A272** runs along the south edge, with Hilltop/Sunset camping zones directly north of it.
- A distinctive white arrow/chevron-shaped clearing appears repeatedly as a landmark near Grand Central/Old Town's path junction — visible at the same relative position (just west of the Oldtown-to-Lion's-Den path) across multiple shots.
