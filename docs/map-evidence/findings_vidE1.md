# vidE part 1 (frames f_0001-f_0099) findings

Video starts fully zoomed out (whole site vs. A31/Alresford Rd/Petersfield Rd), then zooms in through the camping fields (West Camping, Valley Camping, Temple Valley Camping, Tangerine Fields, East Camping, Campervan Field, Meadow Camping — f_0035-f_0075, mostly unlabeled venue-wise) before settling into detailed arena panning from ~f_0057 onward.

**Site-wide orientation** (f_0001, f_0005, f_0010): Arena sits south of a large camping-fields expanse between Alresford Rd (north) and the arena; Petersfield Rd/A272 curves along the arena's south/west edge; A31 crosses further north. West Gate sits at the SW corner of the camping fields with an access track running SE to Downtown; East Gate/South Gate sit on the east side near car parks.

**GRAND CENTRAL** — red-tinted zone, OLDTOWN directly south-southeast of it; large yellow Hilltop camping zone immediately east (f_0045).

**OLDTOWN** — south of Grand Central; in wider shots, directly west of AREA 404 and QUANTUM at similar height (f_0010). An unlabeled pink triangle landmark icon recurs just west of Oldtown (f_0075, f_0084).

**AREA 404** — east of Oldtown, west of Tribe of Frog/Quantum (f_0010). Closer in (f_0086): south of BOTANICA, east of METROPOLIS, north of THE HIDE OUT/DOWNTOWN label. Has a dark animal-silhouette stage icon in a glow halo, with small magenta ribbon decorations around it.

**TRIBE OF FROG** — stage/structure icon; QUANTUM directly east; SUNSET HILL south of both, at edge of woods leading to a yellow hilltop zone (f_0012).

**QUANTUM** — between Tribe of Frog (west) and THE LION'S DEN (east), just west of a big yellow Hilltop wedge (f_0012, f_0057).

**THE LION'S DEN** — orange glow-halo stage icon; east of Quantum/Hilltop zone; low rectangular stage building; pale-blue pond icon just south; adjoins a large pink subdivided camping field to its east (f_0010, f_0015, f_0017, f_0057).

**SUNSET HILL / SUNSET** — Sunset Hill label south of Tribe of Frog/Quantum; a separate "SUNSET" yellow hilltop-camping zone sits further south against Petersfield Rd/A272 (f_0010, f_0012).

**COPPERWOOD** — small reddish callout north of Botanica (f_0075, f_0047).

**BOTANICA** — central arena label; north of Area 404, south of Copperwood, northeast of Metropolis; own dense sub-cluster of illegible stall chips (f_0075, f_0086, f_0047).

**METROPOLIS** — west of Area 404, southwest of Botanica, west of The Hide Out/Downtown (f_0075, f_0086).

**THE HIDE OUT (Downtown)** — two-line callout "THE HIDE OUT / DOWNTOWN" south of Area 404, southeast of Metropolis (f_0086). A separate large "DOWNTOWN" commercial zone also appears further along the pan near West Gate's access road, bordered by a "MEADOW ACCESSIBLE CAMP"/"MEADOW LIVING" zone to its south and Petersfield Rd to its west (f_0072, f_0075).

**Downtown high street path** (f_0090, f_0093) — one continuous winding path, north to south: Soapranos (Laundrette) -> Luck Exchange Casino -> Inconvenience Store -> The Daily Rag -> forks to (a) Hotel Paradiso / Network Comms Station / Postal Posse and (b) Top Up / Blip / Boomtown Bobbies / "Rose An..." (Rose and Clown, cut off).

**NEXUS** — f_0096/f_0098/f_0086: yellow-chip label with a dark mountain/craggy-silhouette stage icon on a glowing green mound. Sits southwest of Botanica Zoo/Karma Ceuticals (north/northeast of it) and south of a "BBXL INFO" kiosk and £ currency-exchange icon. A dashed path runs south from Nexus toward toilet/accessible icons (f_0099).

**Botanica Zoo / Karma Ceuticals / Nanco / Garden Centre** — diagonal stall line NW->SE: Nanco -> Botanica Zoo -> Karma Ceuticals, with Garden Cen[tre] cut off at the right edge roughly level with Karma Ceuticals; whole line sits north/northeast of Nexus (f_0096, f_0098).

**HYDRO XL** — purple glow-halo label east of Petersfield Rd; a "SITE OF SPECIAL SCIENTIFIC INTEREST" green polygon lies directly west of it, between Hydro XL and the road; a lilac dashed loop traces around/behind the stage structure, likely a backstage boundary (f_0069).

**Unlabeled landmarks**: a recurring pale cream kite/arrow shape (likely a large tent/marquee roof outline) appears near West Camping and again near Hotel Paradiso's area, at different pan positions — probably the same landmark type, not a stage.
